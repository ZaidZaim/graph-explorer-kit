# Kestrel Automation — Company Briefing

Kestrel Automation is based in Rotterdam, Netherlands. It sells port automation
software rather than hardware, and positions itself as vendor-neutral: its control
layer runs on top of equipment built by other manufacturers.

## Leadership

Pieter van Dijk is the Chief Executive Officer and a co-founder. He previously ran
terminal operations at the Port of Rotterdam.

Sofia Marchetti is the Chief Technology Officer. She joined from a freight
forwarding platform and rebuilt Kestrel's scheduling engine around a constraint
solver.

## Products

**Harbourmaster** is Kestrel's yard orchestration suite. It assigns equipment to
container moves and uses constraint programming to produce schedules that respect
crane availability and vessel deadlines.

**Kestrel Bridge** is an integration layer that translates between vendor-specific
robot APIs and the Harbourmaster scheduler. Bridge uses an event streaming
architecture to keep yard state current across systems.

## Market position

Kestrel Automation competes with Meridian Robotics, though the two approach the
problem from opposite directions — Kestrel from software, Meridian from hardware.
Analysts have noted that a Kestrel deployment can sit on top of Meridian equipment
without modification, so the rivalry is partial.

Kestrel partners with Nordhavn Terminals for the Rotterdam and Aarhus sites.
