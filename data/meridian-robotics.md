# Meridian Robotics — Company Briefing

Meridian Robotics is an industrial automation company headquartered in Gothenburg,
Sweden, with a secondary engineering office in Tampere, Finland. The company was
founded in 2016 and builds autonomous material-handling robots for warehouse and
port operators.

## Leadership

Ingrid Halvorsen is the Chief Executive Officer. She joined Meridian Robotics in
2019 after eleven years in heavy-equipment manufacturing, and has focused the
company on port logistics rather than the crowded warehouse segment.

Dr. Amara Okonjo leads the perception team as VP of Engineering. Her group is
responsible for the sensor fusion stack that Meridian ships on every unit. Before
Meridian she spent six years at the Chalmers Autonomous Systems Lab.

Tobias Lind is the Head of Product. He owns the roadmap for the Atlas platform and
works directly with port customers on deployment planning.

## Products

The flagship product is the **Atlas Yard Mover**, a container-yard tractor that
operates without a human driver. Atlas relies on LiDAR-based simultaneous
localisation and mapping for navigation, and on a reinforcement learning policy
for close-quarters manoeuvring around stacked containers.

A smaller product, the **Halo Inspection Drone**, performs automated hull and
crane inspections. Halo uses computer vision defect detection built on the same
perception stack as Atlas.

## Partnerships

Meridian Robotics partners with Nordhavn Terminals, the Danish port operator, which
has served as the launch customer for Atlas since 2022. The two companies jointly
operate a test yard in Aarhus.

Meridian also partners with Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg on
sensor research, a relationship that predates the company's founding.

Meridian competes with Kestrel Automation in the North American market.
