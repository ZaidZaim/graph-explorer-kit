# Grand Theft Auto VI — Release, Platforms and Editions

Status key used throughout this corpus: **confirmed** (stated by Rockstar Games,
Take-Two Interactive, or a court record), **reported** (credible press reporting),
**rumored** (fan speculation, datamining, or unverified leak chatter).

## Release

Grand Theft Auto VI is confirmed to release on **Thursday, 19 November 2026**.
Rockstar Games announced this date alongside Take-Two Interactive's quarterly
earnings, moving the game from its previous 26 May 2026 date.

Rockstar's statement was: "Grand Theft Auto VI will now release on Thursday,
November 19, 2026. We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has
been a long wait, but these extra months will allow us to finish the game with the
level of polish you have come to expect and deserve." Status: confirmed.

## Platforms

The game is confirmed for **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X/S**. No PC version
has been announced at launch, following Rockstar's historical pattern of releasing
PC versions later. Status: confirmed for consoles; a launch-day PC release is
rumored only.

The Extended Look gameplay presentation was captured entirely from in-game footage
running on PlayStation 5. Status: confirmed.

## Editions and pre-orders

Pre-orders opened in June 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Status: confirmed.

The **Standard Edition** is priced at $79.99, a ten-dollar increase over the
typical AAA price point. The **Ultimate Edition** is priced at $99.99.
Status: reported.

The Ultimate Edition includes premium content woven through the story: the
**'95 Grotti Cheetah**, the **Shitzu Squalo**, and the **'67 Vapid Dominator
Buggy**, along with personalized weapons, exclusive apparel and tattoos, and
Ultimate-only shops. Status: reported.

Pre-ordering before 20 November 2026 grants the **Vintage Vice City Pack** bonus.
Digital pre-orders additionally include one free month of **GTA+**. Status: reported.

Pre-orders are available through the Rockstar Store, PlayStation Store, Microsoft
Store, and retailers including Amazon, GameStop, Fnac and Micromania. Physical
copies for both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S contain a download code rather
than a disc. Status: reported.
