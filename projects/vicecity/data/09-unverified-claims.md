# Grand Theft Auto VI — Unverified and Disputed Claims

Everything in this document is **rumored**. It circulates widely in fan communities
and is frequently repeated by aggregator sites as though settled. None of it is
confirmed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. It is included so the graph can
answer "what do we actually know?" as distinct from "what is being said?" — the
distinction is the point of tracking evidence level per fact.

## Claimed criminal factions

The **Maddox Syndicate** is described in fan documentation as dealing in money
laundering, real estate fraud and high-level drug distribution — the "clean-suited"
side of Leonida's underworld. Status: rumored. No Rockstar Games source names this
organization.

The **Cuban Connection** is described as controlling **Little Havana** in Vice City,
with legitimate business fronts masking criminal operations. Status: rumored. No
Rockstar Games source names this organization.

An outlaw motorcycle club is claimed to run **meth labs** in the swamps of
**Grassrivers**, sourced to datamining of leaked material. Status: rumored.

## Claimed map figures

Various figures circulate for map size, ranging from 1.7x to more than 2x the area
of Grand Theft Auto V. Claims of **six biomes** and **over 700 interiors** appear
frequently. Status: rumored. Rockstar Games has published no map area, biome count,
or interior count.

## Claimed voice cast

**Manni L. Perez** is widely claimed to voice **Lucia Caminos**. Status: rumored.
Rockstar Games has never announced voice actors before launch for any recent title.

Names from Grand Theft Auto V's cast, including **Ned Luke** and **Roger Clark**,
appear in speculation threads about returning roles. Status: rumored, and
inconsistent with Rockstar Games' stated approach to recasting between entries.

## Claimed radio stations

Beyond the V-Rock logo appearing on merchandise, no radio station has been
announced. Station lineups and track lists circulating online derive from
datamining of leaked material or from fan wishlists. Status: rumored.

## Claimed causal link between the 2022 breach and the delays

Fan discussion frequently attributes the game's delays to the September 2022
Rockstar Games breach. Neither Rockstar Games nor Take-Two Interactive has stated
any such link, and the stated reason for the second delay was development polish.
Status: rumored.

## Claimed PC version at launch

A simultaneous PC release has been claimed repeatedly. Rockstar Games has confirmed
only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Status: rumored.
