# Grand Theft Auto VI — Delay History

Grand Theft Auto VI has been delayed twice from its originally communicated window.

## Original window — Fall 2025

When Rockstar Games released Trailer 1 in **December 2023**, the accompanying
description confirmed a release window of **2025**. Take-Two Interactive's investor
communications clarified this as **Fall 2025**. Status: confirmed.

## First delay — to 26 May 2026

In **May 2025**, Rockstar Games announced the game would not be ready in 2025 and
moved the release to **26 May 2026**. Status: confirmed.

## Second delay — to 19 November 2026

Alongside Take-Two Interactive's July–September quarterly earnings results, the
company announced Grand Theft Auto VI would launch on **19 November 2026** — a
further six-month delay from the May 2026 date. Status: confirmed.

Rockstar Games' statement attributed the additional time to polish: "these extra
months will allow us to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to
expect and deserve." Status: confirmed.

Take-Two CEO **Strauss Zelnick** did not give a specific reason for the delay in his
letter to shareholders, but stated the company remained "both excited and confident
they will deliver an unrivalled blockbuster entertainment experience."
Status: confirmed.

Zelnick separately told **Variety**: "In this instance, of course, we're seeking to
release the most extraordinary title anyone's ever seen in the history of
entertainment." Status: confirmed.

## Cumulative slip

Taken together, the two delays moved the game roughly **fourteen months** from the
originally communicated Fall 2025 window. Take-Two's CEO has publicly characterised
the total slippage at Rockstar Games in terms of an extended development timeline.
Status: reported.
