# Grand Theft Auto VI — Organizations

## Real-world organizations

**Rockstar Games** is the developer of Grand Theft Auto VI. It is a subsidiary of
**Take-Two Interactive**, the publisher. Status: confirmed.

**Take-Two Interactive** is led by CEO **Strauss Zelnick**, who has made repeated
public statements about the game's release timing and commercial expectations.
Status: confirmed.

**Netflix** held a six-hour exclusivity window on the premiere of Grand Theft Auto
VI: An Extended Look on 27 August 2026. Status: confirmed.

**Lapsus$** was the cybercrime group behind the September 2022 Rockstar Games
breach. Its confirmed targets included Rockstar Games, **Uber** and **Nvidia**.
Status: confirmed.

## In-game organizations

**Only Raw Records** is a hip-hop record label founded and run by **Dre'Quan
Priest**, with **Boobie Ike** as investor. It has signed the duo **Real Dimez**.
Status: confirmed.

The **Jack of Hearts** is the strip club owned by Boobie Ike and shown in marketing
material. Status: confirmed.

**V-Rock** is the Vice City rock radio station from the earlier Vice City games. A
redesigned V-Rock logo has appeared on a t-shirt worn by **Jason Duval** and on
merchandise, but Rockstar Games has not formally announced the station's return.
Status: the logo is confirmed; the station's return is rumored.

An **outlaw motorcycle club** operates in the highways and rural regions of Leonida.
A biker convoy has been shown rolling through **Grassrivers**. Status: confirmed
that bikers appear; the club's name and structure are rumored.

## Brand policy

Take-Two Interactive CEO **Strauss Zelnick** has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI
will **not** include real-world product placements. The game will use fictional
parody brands, consistent with the series' established approach — brands such as
**Burger Shot** and **Pißwasser** in earlier entries. Status: confirmed.
