# Grand Theft Auto VI — The State of Leonida

Grand Theft Auto VI is set across the fictional US state of **Leonida**, a version
of Florida, with **Vice City** as its anchor metropolis. Status: confirmed.

The map is substantially larger than Grand Theft Auto V's. Estimates of the exact
multiple vary between roughly 1.7x and 2x the size of the Grand Theft Auto V map;
Rockstar Games has not published a figure. Status: the map is confirmed larger; any
specific multiplier is rumored.

## Vice City

Vice City is the central city of Leonida, returning from Grand Theft Auto: Vice City
(2002) and modelled on Miami. It is the base of operations for **Boobie Ike**,
**Dre'Quan Priest**, and **Only Raw Records**. Status: confirmed.

**Little Havana** is a Vice City district, modelled on the real Miami neighbourhood
of the same name. Status: reported.

## Leonida Keys

The Leonida Keys are a tropical island archipelago in the south of the state,
inspired by the **Florida Keys**, featuring boats, beaches and smuggling routes.
**Jason Duval** worked with drug runners here. Status: confirmed.

## Grassrivers

Grassrivers is Rockstar's version of the **Florida Everglades** — a vast subtropical
wetland of swamps, marshland, slow-moving rivers, cypress forest and isolated rural
communities. A biker convoy has been shown rolling through Grassrivers.
Status: confirmed as a region.

## Port Gellhorn

Port Gellhorn sits on the northwest edge of Leonida. It is a gritty coastal city
once known for tourism and trade, now marked by cheap motels, shuttered attractions,
and an economy of liquor stores, truck stops and underground hustles.
Status: confirmed.

## Ambrosia

Ambrosia is a rural and industrial county defined by sugar refineries, biker gangs
and old-school American grit. It sits near **Lake Leonida**, the large freshwater
body at the centre of the state. Status: confirmed.

## Mount Kalaga National Park

Mount Kalaga National Park is an off-the-grid landmark on the outskirts of Leonida
that draws hunters, fishers and rural residents. Status: confirmed.

## Liberty City

Liberty City is Rockstar's fictional New York, the setting of Grand Theft Auto IV.
It is not a playable area in Grand Theft Auto VI, but **Lucia Caminos** grew up
there before moving to Leonida. Status: confirmed as backstory.
