# Grand Theft Auto VI — Protagonists

Grand Theft Auto VI follows two playable protagonists, **Jason Duval** and
**Lucia Caminos**, a criminal couple. Their attempt at an easy score goes badly
wrong, and the failed job pulls them into a conspiracy stretching across the state
of Leonida, including Vice City. Status: confirmed.

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia Caminos is the **first major female protagonist in mainline Grand Theft Auto
history**. Status: confirmed.

Lucia grew up in **Liberty City** before ending up in the state of Leonida. She was
incarcerated at the **Leonida Penitentiary** for a crime connected to "fighting for
her family"; the specific offence has not been detailed by Rockstar Games.
Status: confirmed that she was incarcerated; the precise charge is reported.

Lucia is romantically and criminally partnered with Jason Duval. The pairing has
been widely compared to a Bonnie-and-Clyde structure. Status: confirmed for the
partnership.

Lucia is rumored to be voiced by **Manni L. Perez**. Rockstar Games has never
officially announced voice actors before launch — it did not for Grand Theft Auto V
or Red Dead Redemption 2 — and the cast list is treated as confidential until the
credits roll. Status: rumored.

## Jason Duval

Jason Duval grew up around grifters and criminals. He served in the **Army** after a
troubled youth, and later took work with drug runners in the **Leonida Keys**.
Status: confirmed.

At the point the story begins, Jason is living in a property owned by
**Brian Heder**, which indicates he is already entangled with the wrong people
before the plot starts. Status: confirmed.

Jason has been shown wearing a t-shirt bearing a redesigned **V-Rock** logo, the
Vice City rock radio station from the earlier Vice City games. Rockstar has shown
the logo on merchandise but has not formally announced V-Rock as a playable
station. Status: the logo is confirmed; the station's return is rumored.

## The partnership

Jason and Lucia are partners whose plans for an easy score go wrong. The dual
protagonist structure is a departure from Grand Theft Auto V's three-protagonist
model and from the single protagonist of earlier entries. Status: confirmed.
