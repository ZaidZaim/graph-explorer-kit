# The September 2022 Rockstar Breach

This document covers the 2022 security breach and the criminal case that followed,
as established in UK court proceedings and contemporaneous reporting. It describes
what happened, who was involved, and the legal outcome. It does not reproduce or
describe the contents of the stolen material.

## The breach

In **September 2022**, an intruder breached **Rockstar Games** and published
approximately **90 clips** of unreleased Grand Theft Auto VI development footage
online. Status: confirmed.

The breach was carried out by **Arion Kurtaj**, then 18 years old, from
**Oxfordshire** in the United Kingdom. Status: confirmed by court finding.

Kurtaj was a member of **Lapsus$**, an international cybercrime group. Status: confirmed.

## The circumstances

Kurtaj carried out the Rockstar Games breach **while in police custody** — under
police protection at a **Travelodge** hotel, having already been arrested in
connection with earlier offences. Status: confirmed.

He conducted the breach using an **Amazon Firestick**, the **television** in his
hotel room, and a **mobile phone**. Status: reported.

## Lapsus$ and the wider campaign

Lapsus$ inflicted an estimated **$10 million** in damages across several
high-profile technology companies. Confirmed targets alongside Rockstar Games
include **Uber** and **Nvidia**. Status: confirmed.

## The prosecution and sentence

Kurtaj was found by a jury to have carried out the offences. Because he was
assessed as not fit to stand trial in the conventional sense, the proceedings
determined whether he committed the acts rather than his criminal intent.
Status: confirmed.

A psychiatric assessment determined that Kurtaj has **acute autism**. The assessment
also found that he remained "highly motivated" and "continued to express the intent
to return to cyber-crime as soon as possible." Status: confirmed.

In **2023**, the judge ordered that Kurtaj be detained under an **indefinite
hospital order** — held in a secure hospital until mental health professionals
determine he no longer poses a risk to the public. Status: confirmed.

## Subsequent developments

Kurtaj has since been released from the secure hospital and is held in a
conventional prison **awaiting retrial**, reported as scheduled for **November**.
Status: reported.

## Effect on the game

Rockstar Games publicly confirmed the breach at the time and stated it did not
expect long-term effect on the development of the game. The 2022 breach is
frequently conflated in fan discussion with the game's later delays; no public
statement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive links the two.
Status: the absence of a stated link is confirmed; any causal connection is rumored.

## Note on leaked material

The material taken in this breach is stolen proprietary content. This corpus models
the breach as a documented event — participants, method, dates, legal outcome — and
deliberately does not catalogue, describe, or reproduce the stolen footage or source
code itself.
