# Grand Theft Auto VI — Marketing Timeline

## Trailer 1 — December 2023

Rockstar Games released the first Grand Theft Auto VI trailer in **December 2023**.
The trailer's description confirmed a release window of **2025**, which Take-Two
Interactive's investor communications later clarified as Fall 2025.
Status: confirmed.

Trailer 1 introduced **Lucia Caminos** and established **Vice City** and the state
of **Leonida** as the setting. Status: confirmed.

## Trailer 2 — May 2025

Rockstar Games released the second trailer in **May 2025**. It showed Vice City in
greater detail and formally introduced **Jason Duval** alongside Lucia, presenting
the two as partners in crime. Status: confirmed.

Trailer 2 also introduced much of the named supporting cast, including
**Cal Hampton**, **Boobie Ike**, **Dre'Quan Priest**, **Real Dimez**, and
**Raul Bautista**. Status: confirmed.

## Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look — 27 August 2026

Rockstar Games premiered **Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look** on
**27 August 2026**. This is the third major marketing beat and by far the longest,
running to roughly 26 minutes of gameplay. Status: confirmed.

The presentation debuted on **Netflix** at 3:00 PM ET. Netflix held a **six-hour
exclusivity window**, after which the Extended Look was published on the official
Rockstar Games YouTube channel and the Grand Theft Auto VI website at 9:00 PM ET on
the same day. Status: confirmed.

The Extended Look was captured entirely from in-game footage running on
**PlayStation 5**. Status: confirmed.

The Netflix exclusivity arrangement was unusual for Rockstar Games, which has
historically premiered trailers on its own YouTube channel, and drew criticism from
parts of the audience for gating the first showing behind a subscription.
Status: reported.

The presentation showed a range of new features and mechanics and represents the
most comprehensive official gameplay showcase of the game to date.
Status: confirmed.

Reporting following the Extended Look indicated the game targets **30 frames per
second** on current consoles. Status: reported.

## Sequence

Trailer 1 (December 2023) preceded Trailer 2 (May 2025), which preceded the
Extended Look (27 August 2026). Three years separate the first trailer from the
Extended Look. Status: confirmed.
