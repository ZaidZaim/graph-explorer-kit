# Grand Theft Auto VI — Supporting Cast

Rockstar Games has named a supporting cast on the official Grand Theft Auto VI
website. The following characters are confirmed to appear.

## Cal Hampton

Cal Hampton is **Jason Duval's** friend, described as easygoing — someone who would
rather stay home with a beer than chase a big score. He is known for eavesdropping
on **Coast Guard** radio chatter. Status: confirmed.

## Boobie Ike

Boobie Ike is a **Vice City** legend who converted his time in the streets into a
legitimate empire spanning **real estate**, a **strip club**, and a **recording
studio**. The strip club shown in marketing material is the **Jack of Hearts**.
Status: confirmed.

Boobie Ike is most invested in his partnership with **Dre'Quan Priest** in the
record label **Only Raw Records**. Status: confirmed.

## Dre'Quan Priest

Dre'Quan Priest is described as more hustler than gangster. He dealt on the streets
to make ends meet, but music was always the goal. He founded and runs
**Only Raw Records**, a hip-hop label. Status: confirmed.

Dre'Quan has signed the duo **Real Dimez** to Only Raw Records and has been booking
acts into Boobie Ike's strip club, while setting his sights on the wider Vice City
music scene. Status: confirmed.

## Real Dimez

Real Dimez is a rap duo made up of **Bae-Luxe** and **Roxy**. The two have been
friends since high school, and turned their time shaking down local dealers into
money through rap tracks and a relentless social media presence. Their early hit
single features local rapper **DWNPLY**. Status: confirmed.

## Raul Bautista

Raul Bautista is a confirmed supporting character. He has been associated in
marketing material with bank robbery crews operating in Leonida.
Status: confirmed as a character; his specific role is reported.

## Brian Heder

Brian Heder owns the property that **Jason Duval** is living in when the story
begins. This connection places Jason inside a criminal network before the plot
starts. Status: confirmed.

## Voice cast

Rockstar Games has confirmed every named character above, but has **not** officially
announced voice actors for any of them. Claims circulating about specific
performers derive from résumé listings, facial-comparison threads, and speculation.
Status: rumored.
