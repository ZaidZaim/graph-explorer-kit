# Meridian Robotics — Company Briefing

Meridian Robotics is an industrial automation company headquartered in Gothenburg,
Sweden. It builds autonomous material-handling robots for port operators.
Status: confirmed.

## Leadership

Ingrid Halvorsen is the Chief Executive Officer. She has focused the company on
port logistics rather than the crowded warehouse segment. Status: confirmed.

Dr. Amara Okonjo leads the perception team as VP of Engineering. Her group owns
the sensor fusion stack that ships on every unit. Status: confirmed.

## Products

The flagship product is the Atlas Yard Mover, a driverless container-yard tractor.
Status: confirmed.

The Halo Inspection Drone performs automated hull and crane inspections.
Status: confirmed.

## Partnerships

Meridian Robotics partners with Nordhavn Terminals, the Danish port operator and
launch customer for Atlas. Status: confirmed.

Meridian competes with Kestrel Automation in the North American market.
Status: reported.
