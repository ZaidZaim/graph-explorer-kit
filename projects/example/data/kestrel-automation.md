# Kestrel Automation — Company Briefing

Kestrel Automation is based in Rotterdam, Netherlands. It sells port automation
software rather than hardware, and positions itself as vendor-neutral.
Status: confirmed.

## Leadership

Pieter van Dijk is the Chief Executive Officer and a co-founder. Status: confirmed.

Sofia Marchetti is the Chief Technology Officer. She rebuilt the scheduling engine
around a constraint solver. Status: confirmed.

## Products

Harbourmaster is Kestrel's yard orchestration suite. It assigns equipment to
container moves. Status: confirmed.

Kestrel Bridge is an integration layer that translates between vendor-specific
robot APIs and the Harbourmaster scheduler. Status: confirmed.

## Market position

Kestrel Automation competes with Meridian Robotics, though the two approach the
problem from opposite directions. Status: reported.

Kestrel partners with Nordhavn Terminals for the Rotterdam site. Status: confirmed.

Analysts claim a Kestrel deployment can run on Meridian equipment without any
modification. Status: rumored.
